# 墨彩纹理生成记录

方式：内置 imagegen。仅生成装饰纹理，界面中的文字、布局和控件不使用生成图。

最终生成提示：

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: decorative ink texture for the outer edge of a Chinese desktop productivity app header, NOT a UI screenshot. Generate a refined abstract black ink-in-water wash on a near-black navy background #0d111b, with iridescent cyan-teal, desaturated indigo and a small touch of luminous violet pigment blooming into the water. A real artistic sumi-e / marbled ink diffusion quality: feathered capillary edges, fine translucent ink veils, subtly granular pigment, organic layered contour filaments. Wide landscape composition, most left area (60%) very dark and nearly empty; the ink bloom is concentrated on the right third and gently dissipates into darkness toward the left. Art-directed, quiet, premium, evocative, technically sophisticated, NOT generic soft gradient blobs, NOT aggressive cyberpunk. Use thin bright pigment edges and restrained glow rather than overexposed clouds. No text, no symbols, no letters, no logos, no interface, no mountains, no stars, no characters. The asset will sit behind header decoration only, and must not dominate a file list. Flat image without device frame or mockup background.

保留原始生成 PNG；预览内嵌的是宽 1200px、质量 82 的 JPEG 编码，以避免在会话预览中请求外部资源。不修改画面内容。
